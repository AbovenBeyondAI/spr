01 / 08

Company Formation

Incorporation for private limited companies, LLPs and partnership firms, handled end to end — name approval, drafting, filing with the Registrar, and the certificate of incorporation.

We confirm your structure is the right fit before filing, so you're not restructuring six months in.

What you'll need

  • PAN and Aadhaar of all directors/partners
  • Proof of registered office address
  • Digital signature certificate for filing directors
  • Proposed company name options
02 / 08

GST Registration & Filing

New GST registration for businesses crossing the threshold, plus ongoing monthly or quarterly return filing and reconciliation so nothing is missed at year-end.

We flag filing deadlines ahead of time rather than after they've passed.

What you'll need

  • PAN of business and proprietor/partners/directors
  • Proof of business address
  • Bank account details
  • Existing sales/purchase records, if already trading
03 / 08

ISO Certification

Gap review against the relevant ISO standard, an action plan to close it, and support through both stages of the certification audit until the certificate is issued.

Includes guidance on the surveillance audits needed to keep the certificate active.

What you'll need

  • Company PAN and GST certificate
  • Certificate of Incorporation (if a company)
  • Company letterhead and visiting card
  • Director/proprietor KYC
  • Staff details — name, designation, gender
04 / 08

Trademark Registration

Trademark search, class selection and filing for your brand name, logo or tagline, with tracking through to registration.

We check for conflicting marks before filing to reduce the chance of objection.

What you'll need

  • Applicant PAN and address proof
  • Logo/wordmark to be registered
  • Business proof, if filing as a company
  • List of goods/services the mark covers
05 / 08

EPF Registration

Employer PF registration and setup, plus ongoing monthly contribution filing once you're registered.

What you'll need

  • Company PAN and incorporation proof
  • Employee count and salary details
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account details
06 / 08

Digital Signature Certificate

Class 3 DSC issuance for e-filing, GST returns, tender submissions and Registrar filings.

What you'll need

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Passport-size photo
  • Active mobile number and email for verification
07 / 08

Import & Export (IEC) Registration

Import Export Code application for businesses that trade across borders — required before you can import or export commercially.

What you'll need

  • PAN of business/proprietor
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account and cancelled cheque
  • Business registration proof
08 / 08

Income Tax Filing

Individual and business income tax return filing, with advance tax planning so you're not caught off guard at year-end.

What you'll need

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 / income statements
  • Bank statements for the financial year
  • Investment and deduction proofs
