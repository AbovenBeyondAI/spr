How it runs

From first call to certificate

The same six stages apply whether you're registering a company or getting ISO certified — only the documents change.

Scope & documentation

We confirm what you need and collect the paperwork specific to your entity type — proprietor, partnership or private limited.

Review

Everything is checked against the current requirement before anything is filed, so applications aren't rejected on avoidable technicalities.

Filing

Your application is submitted to the relevant authority or registrar, with a copy of every filing kept on your file.

Query handling

If the authority raises a query or asks for clarification, we respond directly — you're not the one relaying messages.

Approval & certificate

Once approved, you receive the certificate or registration number along with the underlying documents for your records.

Renewals & follow-up

We flag renewal or filing deadlines ahead of time rather than after they've passed.
