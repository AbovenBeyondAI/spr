About the firm

Built to be one desk, not five specialists.

SPR Consultancy Service was founded in Hosur in 2014, out of a simple frustration business owners kept describing: getting registered and compliant meant coordinating with five different specialists and a dozen follow-up calls, with no one person actually accountable for the whole file.

We built the firm around the opposite idea — company formation, GST, trademark, ISO, EPF, digital signature, import-export and income tax filing, all handled by the same desk, with one person tracking your file from intake to certificate. You call one number and get a straight answer, not a transfer.

Today we operate from two offices — our registered office in Hosur and a second office in Chennai — serving proprietors, partnerships and private limited companies across both cities.
